# Content policy

> **Draft, pending legal review.** This text has not been approved by a lawyer yet. It will be replaced by the reviewed version before Pictora launches.

Pictora is for creative, personal images. This policy says what you can't make or upload, how we check, and what happens when something breaks the rules. It is part of our [terms of service](/terms).

## Not allowed

You may not use Pictora to create, edit or upload:

- **Sexual content involving minors.** Zero tolerance: the account is closed at once and we report it to the authorities where the law requires.
- **Sexual content** of any kind, including nudity in a sexual context.
- **Graphic violence**, gore, or content that promotes or glorifies violence.
- **Hate**: content that attacks or demeans people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
- **Self-harm**: content that encourages or shows suicide, self-injury or eating disorders.
- **Real-person deepfakes in harmful or sexual contexts**: realistic images that put a real person in a sexual, humiliating, violent or misleading situation.
- **Illegal activity**: content that promotes or helps with crimes, such as weapons, drugs, fraud or forged documents.

## Real people

You may upload a photo of a real person, including yourself, and edit it for harmless personal use: a portrait in a new art style, a fun background, a greeting card. Only upload photos you have the right to use, and of people who would be comfortable with the result.

Edits that sexualise, humiliate or impersonate a real person are blocked, as are images meant to deceive others into thinking a real event happened. Don't use Pictora to create images of public figures that could mislead people, for example in news, politics or advertising.

## How we check

Every prompt, and every image you upload, is checked by an automated moderation system before any image is made. The prompt enhancer's rewrite is checked again, and the image models apply their own safety filters. If a request is blocked, no points are used and you see a short explanation. Blocked requests are logged with the prompt and the reason, and kept for 90 days so that our team can review them.

Automated checks make mistakes in both directions. If you think a block was wrong, tell us at [{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}).

## Consequences

- **Cooldown:** 3 blocked requests within 24 hours pause your account for 24 hours.
- **Suspension:** repeated or serious violations lead to suspension while an admin reviews the account, and may lead to the account being closed.
- **Termination:** sexual content involving minors closes the account immediately.

We may also remove content and lower an account's daily allowance when we see signs of abuse, as the [terms of service](/terms) describe.

## How to report

If Pictora produces an image that breaks this policy, use **Report** in the image's detail view. Reports go to our review queue with the image's prompt and settings.

To report anything else, such as an image of you that you believe someone made with Pictora, email [{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}) with as much detail as you can.
